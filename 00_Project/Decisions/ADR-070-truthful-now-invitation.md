# ADR-070 — A relevant, truthful invitation on Nu

Status: Accepted by Founder on 2026-09-25
Version: 1.0

## Authority and scope

After the ADR-069 delivery, the proposed next step was Nu: more personal,
relevant and without an invented start time. The Founder replied “Ga verder”.
This records that bounded continuation, not acceptance of untested iPhone flows.
Roadmap phases 4/9; roles Product Guardian, Engineer, Reviewer.

Amend ADR-067's Nu inventory: replace the arbitrary start clock and exact match
number with an invitation to view the experience, its existing promise, and
short reasons based on explicit preferences or clearly labelled capsule facts.
The ranking engine is unchanged. No inferred health, mood or free-time claims.

Keep photography, layered materials, serif typography, company control,
finite alternatives and the existing guide/resume path.
No default place is presented as the user's location. No generic “nearby”
or walking-distance claim without verified context.
Weather is secondary and only for outside/route/handoff activities, with
confirmed environment, a live weather receipt and a valid unexpired snapshot.
Without that evidence, show an honest low-context state. Remove the generic
sun countdown and estimated light chart from Nu, not replace them with invented
live facts. Existing source adapters, permission flows and model triggers stay unchanged.

No new sources, native dependencies, storage migration, health access,
background location, ranking rewrite or Dag redesign. Existing gaps in other
screens and contextual selection are not silently declared solved.

## Acceptance

No “Start om”, numerical match or default Dokkum without confirmed context.
Workout remains useful without weather; a chosen interest is called a
preference, estimated duration is not free-calendar time. Expired/future/invalid
weather inputs fail closed. CTA still opens matching Voorpret; resume and
finite alternatives remain functional. Existing tests, new regressions and
exports precede existing private iOS preview distribution. Native acceptance
is separate; rollback remains the ADR-069 preview update.
